Small tool, real problem

Stop letting the next step disappear.

For freelancers and small service businesses, a missed follow-up can mean a lost opportunity. RelayDesk makes the next action visible.

A simple daily loop

Three steps. Less mental clutter.

01 / CAPTURE

Add the conversation

Record the person, the opportunity, and what they need. Keep it short enough to use every day.

02 / DECIDE

Choose a next action

Set a date and status so every active opportunity has a sensible next step.

03 / FOLLOW THROUGH

Send a human message

Draft a relevant follow-up, personalize it, and keep the conversation moving.
