Real systems are connected. Mindmesh gives you a small, inspectable place to explore those connections without pretending the model is a crystal ball.

Build a living system

Keep the experiment yours

The Mindmesh principle

A model is a question you can execute.

Mindmesh is an experimental systems sandbox, not a source of guaranteed predictions. You define the rules; the simulator makes their consequences easier to inspect. Start simple, challenge your assumptions, and refine the model.
